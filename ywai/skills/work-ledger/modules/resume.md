# Resume

After compaction, a summary, or a session boundary, the ledger survives; the premise of the work does not. Before you touch the work:

1. Re-read the ledger in full — every verified entry, not just the last one.
2. Re-read the goal line by line.
3. State the pass (`solo` | `thin` | `full`).
4. Make `Next` the first action back.

`ywai ledger resume` prints the ledger and those four steps. Without the CLI, the four steps are the whole protocol.

If there is no ledger, do not invent one. Classify again from the user's current request.
